## Content Brief: marketing retainer cost South Africa

### Search Intent
Commercial and transactional. Searchers want a number and what it buys, then want to know what to watch for in the contract. Google rewards cost-guide articles with ranges and a comparison table. Audience: South African owner or office manager pricing a first agency; low jargon tolerance.

### Competitor Analysis
| # | URL | Key H2 sections | Est. words | Score | Main gap |
|---|-----|-----------------|------------|-------|----------|
| 1 | preferredmarketers.co.za (monthly pricing + what's included) | cost by service, what's included (headings were not machine-readable) | ~1,360 | 24/40 | No table, no FAQ schema; ranges without a worked example |
| 2 | juicydesigns.co.za (retainer vs project vs hourly) | four models at a glance, retainers, projects, hourly, % of ad spend, choosing, FAQ | ~1,420 | 31/40 | Compares models but gives few rand figures |
| 3 | mintt.co.za (marketing retainer SA) | what is a retainer, what it includes, is it right for you | ~590 | 16/40 | Thin; no prices at all |
| 4 | simboti.digital / syte.co.za / honeywhale.co.za (listed, not read) | not read | n/a | n/a | n/a |

### Content Gaps and Opportunities
- **Topic gap:** none of the three pages I read states contract terms (minimum term, notice period) next to price. A search result summary says SA PR retainers commonly lock in 6–12 months; this is a vendor-sourced claim, **UNCONFIRMED**.
- **Depth gap:** ranges are given without showing what a given rand amount actually produces in a month.
- **Quality gap:** no dates on figures; no "what is not included" list (ad spend, stock images, tools).
- Market ranges seen in search results (vendor-reported, mixed sources): roughly R5,000–R35,000/month for small-business retainers, R10,000–R50,000+ for multi-channel, SEO R3,000–R20,000+, social packages from about R3,500. Re-check each source before publishing.
