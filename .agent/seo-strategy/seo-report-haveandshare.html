Keyword Usage & Density
▾

Zero geo-modifiers on most pages

"South Africa", "Johannesburg", and "Gauteng" appear sporadically — only the Corporate Facilitation page mentions "South African" once in body text. All other pages have zero location keywords despite being a location-dependent service.

→ Add location modifier to: page title, H1, first paragraph, one H2, meta description, and footer — on every page. This is the single highest-impact fix for local organic search.
Critical

"Claudina Hafenscher" not used as a keyword

The coach's name (a navigational keyword with zero competition) does not feature in any page title or meta description despite being the brand's most unique identifier. People who've seen Claudina speak, or been referred to her, will Google her name.

→ Add "Claudina Hafenscher" to homepage title, About page title, and meta descriptions. This captures branded + referral traffic with no difficulty.
High

"COMENSA Accredited" only on service sub-pages

The COMENSA accreditation is a significant trust signal and search differentiator — but it only appears in the "Meet Your Coach" section on individual pages, not on the homepage or in any title/meta. People actively search for COMENSA-accredited coaches.

→ Add "COMENSA Accredited Senior Coach" to the homepage About section and a dedicated credentials line in the footer. Add it to the About page title.
Medium

PQ® page keyword density is well-balanced

"Positive Intelligence", "PQ", "mental fitness", "Saboteur", and "Sage" all appear multiple times naturally. Good semantic saturation for the topic.

Pass

mBIT and Educational Kinesiology pages not audited — likely thin

Based on patterns from other pages, the methodology pillar pages likely have same geo-gap issues. These pages represent very low-competition keyword categories in SA.

→ Audit each pillar page. Add 300+ words of original, geo-targeted content per pillar. "mBIT coach South Africa" has virtually zero competition — own it.

Internal Linking Structure
▾

Good cross-linking between methodology pillars

The Corporate Facilitation page links to mBIT and Educational Kinesiology. The PQ® page links to Reality Check™ and Corporate Facilitation. Solid foundation.

Pass

Blog posts not linked from service pages

The Individual Coaching page links to "Mental Fitness With PQ Coaching" and "Introduction to mBIT Coaching" blog posts — but most service pages don't cross-link to relevant blog content. This is a missed topical authority signal.

→ Add a "Further Reading" or "Related Insights" section at the bottom of every service page, linking to 2–3 relevant blog articles. This deepens topical authority signals.
High

No "About Claudina" page appears in navigation

The About section is an anchor on the homepage (#about) — not a standalone page. Google cannot index or rank a section within a page for branded queries like "About Claudina Hafenscher coach".

→ Create a standalone /about.html page with its own title tag, H1, and 800+ words of content including credentials, certifications, career story, and philosophy.
High

Contact page routes to TidyCal (external domain)

"Contact Us" in the footer links to tidycal.com — an external domain. This loses PageRank that should flow to an internal /contact.html page. Also, Google can't track conversions from this flow without additional UTM setup.

→ Create /contact.html with contact form + embedded booking widget. Keep TidyCal as the backend, but keep users on-domain.

⚙️
Technical SEO
▾

No schema markup detected on any page

Zero JSON-LD or Microdata schema found across all 11 crawled pages. This means no eligibility for rich results: no local business knowledge panel, no FAQ rich snippets, no review stars, no breadcrumbs in search results.

→ Priority: Add LocalBusiness + Person schema on homepage. Add Service schema on each service page. Add FAQPage schema on pages with FAQ sections. Full schema code provided in the Schema tab.
Critical

No sitemap.xml confirmed

A sitemap.xml was not discoverable during the audit. Without a sitemap, Google may not find or prioritise indexing new or updated pages.

→ Generate sitemap.xml listing all 11 pages + any blog articles. Submit via Google Search Console.
Critical

No robots.txt confirmation

robots.txt not confirmed. Google needs this file to understand crawl permissions.

→ Create /robots.txt with: User-agent: * Allow: / Sitemap: https://haveandshare.co.za/sitemap.xml
High

HTTPS confirmed

Site is served over HTTPS — a baseline Google ranking signal. ✓

Pass

No Google My Business integration signals

Physical address appears in footer (13 Morester Glen Marais, Gauteng) but no NAP (Name/Address/Phone) schema confirms this for Google's local algorithm. Google My Business listing not verified in audit.

→ (1) Verify/create Google Business Profile for "Have and Share Coaching". (2) Add NAP schema to footer on every page. (3) Match NAP exactly across all online citations.
High

CTA clicks out to third-party domain

Every "Book a Session" CTA routes to tidycal.com. This breaks the user journey domain continuity and means Google sees the conversion happening off-site — no signal back to haveandshare.co.za.

→ Either embed TidyCal on an internal /book.html page, or at minimum add UTM parameters to all TidyCal links to enable tracking via Google Analytics.
Medium

Open Graph images not confirmed

OG descriptions are present but OG images were not confirmed. Without OG images, social shares will display blank previews — significantly reducing click-through from social referrals.

→ Create one branded OG image per major page (1200×630px). Set <meta property="og:image"> and <meta name="twitter:image"> on every page.
Medium
